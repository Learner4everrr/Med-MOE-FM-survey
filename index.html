Review methods

Transparent by design

This public evidence map presents bibliographic metadata and structured study characteristics from a PRISMA-ScR-aligned review. It does not redistribute article abstracts, full-text passages, or PDFs.

01

Comprehensive discovery

Seven bibliographic databases and three supplementary discovery sources.

02

Structured study profiles

Study characteristics and evidence attributes are presented in a consistent format.

03

Operational taxonomy

Applications, expert organization, routing, MoE function, and evidence were coded separately.

04

Reproducible public view

The website is generated from a versioned, machine-readable JSON dataset.
